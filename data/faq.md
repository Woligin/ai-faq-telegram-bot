# Opening hours
We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 and on Saturday from 10:00 to 15:00. We are closed on Sunday.

# Delivery
Delivery inside the city takes 1 to 2 working days and costs 5 USD. Orders above 50 USD are delivered for free. International delivery takes 7 to 14 days.

# Returns
You can return an item within 14 days if it is unused and in its original package. Refunds are made to the original payment method within 5 working days after we receive the item.

# Payment
We accept bank cards and PayPal. Payment is taken when the order is confirmed.

# Contacts
Write to support@example.com. We answer within one working day.
